# Computer Vision

Computer vision is a field of AI that teaches computers to comprehend, identify and interpret objects, people and scenes in images and videos.

## The pipeline

1. Image acquisition: the image is captured using a camera or sensor
2. Image processing: the image is processed and enhanced (noise removal, contrast adjustment, resizing)
3. Segmentation: the image is divided into multiple sections or regions of interest
4. Pre processing of the segmented image: the regions are cleaned and prepared for analysis
5. Feature extraction: specific data is extracted from the image, such as edges, shapes, colours and textures
6. Feature based classification: the extracted features are used to classify or recognise the objects in the image

## Applications

1. **Healthcare**: pathology, diagnostic imaging (X rays, MRI, CT scans), dermatology (skin lesion analysis)
2. **Autonomous vehicles**: Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), lane and obstacle detection, licence plate recognition
3. **Retail and e commerce**: visual search, checkout free stores (for example Amazon Go)
4. **Manufacturing**: automated quality control and defect detection
