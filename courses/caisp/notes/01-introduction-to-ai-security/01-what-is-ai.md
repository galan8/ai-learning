# What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science focused on building machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, understanding language, recognising patterns, reasoning and making decisions.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI in which computers learn from data rather than being given detailed step by step instructions, unlike traditional programming.

## Narrow AI (Weak AI)

Handles one specific task or a narrow set of tasks, for example voice recognition, image analysis, recommendation systems, virtual assistants (Siri, Google Assistant) or self driving features.

Limited scope and focus: it does not truly understand what it is doing and has no consciousness or general reasoning ability. All AI in use today is Narrow AI.

**Advantages:**

1. Faster and more accurate completion of specific tasks
2. Available 24 by 7 to perform the desired tasks
3. Minimises human error through automation
4. Can perform tasks that are dangerous or impractical for humans

## Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

A theoretical form of machine intelligence that mimics human cognition: it can understand, learn and solve complex problems across any domain, replicating human thinking and behaviour rather than a single skill.

**Key capabilities that define AGI:**

1. Abstract thinking: the ability to work with concepts, for example reasoning about justice or freedom
2. Background knowledge: broad general knowledge across many topics, similar to a human
3. Common sense: making sensible decisions and understanding everyday situations
4. Cause and effect: understanding that different actions lead to different outcomes
5. Transfer learning: applying knowledge learned in one area to a completely different area

**What AGI would be able to achieve:**

1. Read, understand and write code
2. Recognise colours and perceive depth (visual perception)
3. Handle complex physical tasks requiring fine motor skills
4. Understand human intent and language in different contexts (natural language understanding, intuition)
5. Navigate physical environments

Note: AGI does not exist yet; it remains a research goal.
